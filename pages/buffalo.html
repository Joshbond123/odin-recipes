Buffalo Chicken and Roasted

HOME

DISCRIPTION

Buffalo chicken and roasted potatoes is a simple and flavorful meal made with tender chicken
coated in spicy buffalo sauce and crispy roasted potatoes.
It is easy to prepare and works well for lunch or dinner.

INGRIDIENT

  • Chicken breasts
  • Buffalo sauce
  • Olive oil
  • Garlic powder
  • Paprika
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Potatoes
  • Onion powder

STEPS

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F)
  2. Wash and cut the potatoes into small pieces.
  3. Season the potatoes with olive oil, garlic powder, paprika, salt, and black pepper.
  4. Place the potatoes on a baking tray and roast for about 30–40 minutes until golden and tender.
  5. Season and cook the chicken until fully cooked.
  6. Coat the chicken with buffalo sauce
  7. Serve the buffalo chicken with the roasted potatoes.
