Rice And stew

 HOME

DISCRIPTION

Rice and stew is a popular and comforting meal made with fluffy cooked rice served with
a rich tomato and pepper stew. The stew can be enjoyed with chicken, beef, or on its own.

INGRIDIENT

  • Rice
  • Tomatoes
  • Red bell peppers
  • Scotch bonnet peppers
  • Onions
  • Tomato paste
  • Vegetable oil
  • Salt
  • Seasoning cube

STEPS

  1. Wash and cook the rice until it is soft and tender.
  2. Blend the tomatoes, peppers, and onions.
  3. Heat oil in a pot and fry some chopped onions.
  4. Add the tomato paste and blended pepper mixture.
  5. Season with salt and seasoning cube, then cook until the stew thickens.
  6. Add cooked chicken or beef if desired.
  7. Serve the stew with the cooked rice.
